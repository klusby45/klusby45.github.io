How it works
  1. Each round hides a number, like the sum of 5 dice. Pieces get revealed one at a time.
  2. You quote a bid (you buy) and an ask (you sell), no wider than the max.
  3. One trader shows up per quote. Noise traders trade randomly and love tight markets. Informed traders peeked at a hidden piece and only trade when you're wrong.
  4. You quote twice per reveal. If someone lifts your offer, maybe they know something.
  5. The contract settles at the true value. Position plus cash is your P&L.
How scoring works

P&L is what you actually made, luck included. Edge is the expected value of every quote you made, given everything you'd seen at that point (reveals and trades). Edge is your skill score. Luck is the gap. You can lose money and still post a big edge.
