Market Maker
← Back to Kyle's page

Make the market. Don't get picked off.

You're a trader making markets on a hidden number. Some people trading with you know nothing. Some know more than you do. Your job is to price it right and figure out which is which.

  1. Each round has a hidden number, like the sum of 5 dice. Items get revealed one at a time.
  2. You quote a bid (what you'd buy at) and an ask (what you'd sell at). Width can't exceed the max shown.
  3. One trader shows up per quote. Noise traders trade randomly and like tight markets. Informed traders have peeked at one hidden item and only trade when your price is wrong.
  4. You quote twice per reveal, so watch the flow. If someone lifts your offer, maybe they know something.
  5. At the end, the contract settles at the true value. Your position plus cash is your P&L.
How scoring works

You get two numbers. P&L is what you actually made, luck included. Edge is the expected value of every quote you made, given everything you'd seen up to that point (reveals and trades). Edge is your skill score. Luck is the gap between them. A great player can lose money on a session and still post a big edge.
